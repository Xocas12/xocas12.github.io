The 1956 target list

In June 1956 the Strategic Air Command wrote down the places it would need to destroy, and ranked them. All 2,339 plottable entries of the declassified study, transcribed in nuclear-probabilities and drawn at its own coordinates. There is no basemap: every dot is a target, and what they draw is the Soviet urban system as the planners saw it, down to the Trans-Siberian strung out across the east. Pull the tier slider and the plan concentrates westward. The point of the panel underneath is that you can check it — it prints the line as the page prints it, and links to the scan.
