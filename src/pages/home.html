About

I work on computational approaches to social, economic and political questions, mostly to understand what makes governance work. In practice that means data engineering and statistics.

Right now I'm the sole engineer at Samford Group, where I built a platform that screens about 1.6 million US parcels for land acquisition. Before that I did an MS in Information Technology Strategy at Carnegie Mellon, working on an AI-supported wargaming platform and on research into who sets the political agenda online. I studied data science and AI at Leiden.

On the side I run Vixía, an OSINT map of the war in Ukraine, and build research tools: statistical tests for manipulated election results, a multi-agent model of Soviet planning and a dataset of Cold War nuclear targets. I also write policy memos on AI governance, arms control and cyber policy.

I've competed in judo, jiu-jitsu, sailing, history bees and math competitions. I speak English, Spanish and Galician, plus German and some Portuguese.

The rest: CV, projects, writing, email.
