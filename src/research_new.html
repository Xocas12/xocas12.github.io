battlelab · Monte Carlo battle analysis

open source

A Monte Carlo framework for analysing why a battle went the way it did. Two engines share one analysis layer: a native Python model (zones, units, a fixed turn sequence) and a Lua harness that runs replications of a Command: Modern Operations scenario from the same parameter draws into the same CSV schema. A 48-hour replication takes about 3.5 ms.

Every parameter sits in the scenario file with a distribution, a source and a confidence, and battlelab lint refuses to run a scenario with an unsourced parameter that isn't marked as an assumption. Scenarios record what actually happened, and the tool reports where that falls in the model's distribution. Combat resolution is swappable, so a conclusion can be checked against the modelling choice. Draws depend only on seed, run and parameter name (common random numbers), which keeps factor swaps and Shapley decomposition stable at modest run counts.

Eucalyptus in Galicia · fire, forest and water

open source · preliminary

Estimates the effect of eucalyptus plantations on native forest, wildfire and water in Galicia. Plantations are mapped from satellite land cover (442,209 ha in 2024) and checked against national forest inventory plots. Estimation uses double/debiased ML with spatial cross-fitting and block-clustered errors, SIMEX for map error, and Cinelli-Hazlett sensitivity bounds.

  • Fire, 2018 to 2023, 29,565 grid cells: ten more points of eucalyptus cover is associated with a 0.26-point lower annual burn probability relative to farmland and other cover (95% CI −0.46 to −0.07). Relative to native broadleaf the estimate is +0.23 points and the interval includes zero.
  • Conversion: about 100 ha of native forest became eucalyptus between 2017 and 2024 among confidently mapped pixels, 89 ha of it corroborated by tree-cover loss or fire.

Neither fire estimate is robust; a weak unmeasured confounder could erase the first. The water effect couldn't be measured, and a Landsat back-cast that failed its validation gate was dropped. The maps are checked against a 1998 forest map; the check against the 2011 edition is still to run.
