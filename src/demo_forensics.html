Integer-percentage turnout test

When officials are given a turnout target, reported turnout lands on whole percentages more often than counting noise allows. This is the precinct data from Russia's 2011 Duma and 2018 presidential elections, run through the test as implemented in forensic-elections. Bars are precincts per 0.1-point bin, amber bars are whole percentages, and the line is the count expected under binomial noise.
