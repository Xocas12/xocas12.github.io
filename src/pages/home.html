Xocas Álvarez

Data and ML engineer, Barcelona

I work on computational approaches to social, economic and political questions, with the aim of understanding what makes governance work. Most of it is data engineering and statistics applied to elections, finance, conflict and public records.

Right now I'm the sole engineer at Samford Group, where I built a platform that screens about 1.6 million US parcels for land acquisition. Before that I did an MS in Information Technology Strategy at Carnegie Mellon, working on an AI-supported wargaming platform and on research into who sets the political agenda online. I studied data science and AI at Leiden.

Outside of work I build research tools: statistical tests for manipulated election results, a multi-agent model of Soviet planning, a dataset of Cold War nuclear targets and Vixía, an OSINT map of the war in Ukraine. I also write policy memos on AI governance, arms control and cyber policy.

I've competed in judo, jiu-jitsu, sailing and history bees. I speak English, Spanish and Galician, plus German and some Portuguese.
