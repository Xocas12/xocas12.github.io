gosplan-env · planning incentives and misreporting

open source · in progress

Multi-agent RL environment where firms answer to a Soviet-style planner that sets targets, audits reports and raises targets after a good year. It tests whether misreporting emerges from those incentives alone. Two pre-registered gates failed, so later results are exploratory.
