Bunching at the plan notch

Twenty learning enterprises face a planner that pays a bonus for hitting the plan. Nothing in the reward tells them to report exactly 1.00, and under a sharp notch a fifth of every report they ever file lands in one bin just above it. Widen the notch and the pile does not merely shrink — it walks off the target, because a wide notch is a plateau and the mass spreads along it. These are 1.9 million reported fulfilment ratios from trained populations, with the overfulfilment cap held fixed so the notch is the only thing moving. The repository counts this as a pipeline check rather than a finding, and its pre-registered test for it failed.
