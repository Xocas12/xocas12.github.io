Where it grows is not where it burns

Galicia at one square kilometre, 29,565 cells. Eucalyptus is a dense band hugging the Atlantic coast and the north; the fires of 2018–2023 fall in the drier interior, and the two barely overlap. Bin the cells by eucalyptus fraction and the burnt share falls almost monotonically, 4.5 per cent down to 0.3. That is not an effect — plantations sit on the wet, mild coast, which differs from the interior in a dozen ways besides eucalyptus.
