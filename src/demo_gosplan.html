Bunching at the plan notch

Twenty learning enterprises face a planner that pays a bonus for hitting the plan. Nothing in the reward tells them to report exactly 1.00, and under a sharp notch a fifth of every report they ever file lands in one bin just above it. Smooth the notch and reporting exactly 1.00 earns only half of it. The median report moves from 1.007 under the sharp notch to 1.043 at width 0.02, where about nine-tenths of the notch is already paid, and 1.077 at 0.05, then comes back to 1.066 at 0.1 and 0.981 at 0.25. By 0.25 the notch is a gentle ramp, and 54 per cent of reports sit below the plan, 36 per cent below 0.90. These are 1.9 million reported fulfilment ratios from trained populations, with the overfulfilment cap held fixed so the notch is the only thing moving. The repository counts this as a pipeline check rather than a finding, and its pre-registered test for it failed.
