Gaming a plan bonus

From gosplan-env, my simulation of Soviet planning: twenty learning enterprises and a planner that pays a bonus for hitting the plan. With a narrow bonus window, a fifth of all reports land in a single bin just above 100%. Widen the window and the pile spreads out and drifts off target. 1.9 million reports from trained agents; only the window width changes between settings.
